## One of the fastest-growing ingredient categories

According to Research and Markets, the global freeze-dried fruit powder market grew from **US$6.25 billion in 2025** to **US$6.93 billion in 2026**, and is forecast to reach **US$13.58 billion by 2032** — a compound annual growth rate of roughly **11.7%**, notably higher than the 5–6% average growth of many other food categories. Other reports define the market more broadly (including whole freeze-dried fruit pieces) and arrive at much larger figures — the difference mainly comes down to how product scope is defined.

## Why is it growing so fast?

The most commonly cited driver across reports is rising demand for **clean-label ingredients** (simple, additive-free composition) and **functional foods** — freeze-dried fruit powder retains nearly all of a fruit's vitamins, color and natural flavor compared to other drying methods, which fits well with consumer demand for "real," health-supportive ingredients.

The most common application remains beverage/smoothie powders, followed by bakery/confectionery and nutraceuticals — closely matching the applications Whitehorse Foodtech's fruit powder line (dragon fruit, mango, passion fruit, soursop, durian) already serves.

## What this means for importers

With double-digit growth, having a supply source that's both stable and able to scale volume matters more each year — one more reason for buyers to prioritize working with suppliers that have real processing capability (not just trading intermediaries), so supply can keep up as demand accelerates over the next year or two.
