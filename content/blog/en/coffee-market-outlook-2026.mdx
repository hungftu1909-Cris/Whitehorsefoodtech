## Market size and growth rate

According to Grand View Research, the global coffee market reached **US$249.3 billion in 2025** and is projected to grow to **US$263.5 billion in 2026**, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of roughly **5.4%** through 2033, reaching an estimated $380.3 billion.

Worth noting: different research firms publish fairly different market-size figures (due to different scope definitions and methodologies — some include retail/foodservice channels, others count raw material trade only). That said, most reports agree on a **growth rate in the 5–5.5% per year** range, pointing to a stable growth trajectory for the industry.

## Three trends worth watching in 2026

**1. Premium coffee continues to drive growth.** Consumers are increasingly willing to pay more for traceable, specialty-grade coffee rather than commodity-grade blends.

**2. Ready-to-drink (RTD) and cold brew are growing fast.** This is one of the most frequently cited growth drivers across reports, aligned with convenience-focused consumption trends.

**3. Sustainability and traceability matter more** in purchasing decisions for both end consumers and importing businesses.

## What this means for coffee buyers

Given fast growth in premium and RTD coffee, importers may want to diversify beyond traditional green/roasted coffee — for example soluble coffee powder, liquid extract or cold brew concentrate — to keep pace with market demand. This is part of why a number of Vietnamese suppliers, including Whitehorse Foodtech, have expanded into deeper-processed coffee lines alongside traditional green beans.
